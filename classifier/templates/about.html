About the Hyperspectral Image Classification Project

🎯 Objective

Classify land-cover types from hyperspectral imagery using a deep learning-based encoder-decoder model.

🧠 Model Overview

Our 3D CNN captures spatial and spectral information jointly, providing high accuracy on benchmark datasets.

📊 Dataset Information

  • Indian Pines: 145×145 pixels, 200 bands, 16 classes
  • Pavia University: 610×340 pixels, 103 bands, 9 classes
  • Salinas: 512×217 pixels, 204 bands, 16 classes
